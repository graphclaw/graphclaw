# Reply Tone Policy

Controls the agent's writing voice when drafting outbound messages.

## Voice options

- `first_person`: "I will follow up on this tomorrow." (recommended for personal comms)
- `third_person`: "The team will follow up on this tomorrow."
- `neutral`: Avoids first/third; uses passive or impersonal constructions.

## Brevity

- `terse`: 1–2 sentences max.
- `medium`: Up to one short paragraph.
- `verbose`: Full context and reasoning included.

## Emoji

emoji_allowed = false keeps all outbound messages text-only.
